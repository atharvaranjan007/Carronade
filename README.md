# 📖 About Carronade

Carronade is an intelligent agricultural protection system designed to protect crops from birds and wild animals through **automated detection and non-lethal acoustic deterrence**.

Farmers around the world face recurring crop losses caused by animals such as wild boars, monkeys, birds, and other wildlife. Traditional methods including scarecrows, manual guarding, fencing, and conventional deterrents can be expensive, labour-intensive, unreliable, or require continuous human intervention.

Carronade addresses this challenge by combining **intelligent animal detection, adaptive acoustic deterrence, embedded systems, solar power, GPS, and remote monitoring** into a single agricultural protection platform.

Instead of physically harming animals, Carronade detects potential intrusions and responds with carefully selected deterrent sounds designed to encourage animals to leave the protected area.

The system is designed to operate autonomously, reducing the need for farmers to continuously monitor their fields while helping protect crops and promote safer human-wildlife coexistence.

# ❗ Problem Statement

Agricultural fields are frequently affected by birds and wild animals that damage crops, reduce yields, and create significant financial losses for farmers.

Farmers commonly rely on methods such as:

* Manual night guarding
* Scarecrows
* Physical fencing
* Flashlights
* Conventional sound deterrents
* Chemical repellents

However, these approaches have several limitations.

Manual monitoring requires significant time and labour. Scarecrows and static deterrents can become ineffective when animals become accustomed to them. Physical barriers can be expensive to install and maintain, while certain chemical methods may negatively affect the surrounding environment.

There is therefore a need for an **affordable, automated, intelligent, and environmentally responsible crop-protection system** that can detect animal activity and respond without physically harming wildlife.

**Carronade is designed to address this gap.**

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Carronade is a smart acoustic crop-protection system that combines **animal detection, intelligent decision-making, acoustic deterrence, solar energy, GPS, and remote monitoring**.

The system continuously monitors the agricultural field using sensors and an intelligent camera-based detection system.

When potential animal activity is detected, Carronade identifies the intrusion and activates an appropriate acoustic deterrent.

The system can also provide information to the farmer through a connected monitoring interface, allowing farmers to understand activity occurring in their fields without being physically present.
